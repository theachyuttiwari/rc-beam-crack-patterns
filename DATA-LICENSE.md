# Data licence

The images in `data/panels/` are crops of figures from open-access journal articles. Each article is published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The images in this repository are released under the same licence, CC BY 4.0.

## What you must do

You must give credit to the original authors of each article when you reuse an image. `data/SOURCES.md` lists, for every article, the citation, the DOI link and the licence. `data/manifest.csv` also carries the citation and licence of each image in its `citation` and `licence` columns.

You must indicate that changes were made. The only changes made here are cropping a figure to one beam panel and, for some figures, splitting a composite figure into several panels. Colour, resolution and content are not altered.

The licence does not apply to the labels, quotes and metadata we added. Those are released under CC BY 4.0 as well. The evidence quotes are short verbatim extracts from the cited articles, kept as evidence for each label.

The source code is separate. It is released under the MIT licence (see `LICENSE`).

## Not included

The Davoudi et al. crack-map dataset (Mendeley Data, DOI 10.17632/vkgw6gg8v3.1, CC BY 4.0) is not redistributed here. See `data/README.md` for how to get it.
